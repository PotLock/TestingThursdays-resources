# Fake-Door Tests

A fake-door test measures interest in a feature or workflow before implementation.

## Ethical use

- avoid materially misleading people;
- clearly handle the click with an honest “not available yet / join research” state;
- do not fake consequential financial, health, security, or account behavior;
- use the signal to decide whether deeper research is warranted.

## Record

- audience;
- exposure count;
- clicks/intent;
- target-user fit;
- follow-up interview acceptance;
- decision.
