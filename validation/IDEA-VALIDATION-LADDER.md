# Idea Validation Ladder

Use the cheapest credible evidence before committing to expensive implementation.

1. **Problem conversations** — does the problem actually occur?
2. **Existing behavior** — what do people do today?
3. **Commitment** — will they give time, data, access, or another meaningful signal?
4. **Landing page / smoke test** — does the message attract qualified interest?
5. **Prototype** — can people understand the proposed interaction?
6. **Concierge MVP** — can you deliver the outcome manually?
7. **MVP behavior** — can users reach value end-to-end?
8. **Retention** — do they return?
9. **Payment** — will the relevant buyer pay?

## Before writing code, ask

- Who has this problem?
- How frequently?
- How painful/valuable is it?
- What do they do now?
- Why would they switch?
- How will we reach them?
- What is the riskiest assumption?
- Can we test that assumption in a day?
