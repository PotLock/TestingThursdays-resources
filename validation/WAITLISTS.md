# Waitlists as Experiments

A waitlist is not proof of product-market fit. Treat it as a prelaunch funnel.

```text
visitor → waitlist → qualified → interview → invite → activated → retained → paid
```

## Useful fields

- contact information;
- target-user screener;
- what they are trying to accomplish;
- current alternative/workaround;
- willingness to participate in early testing.

## Measure

- signup source;
- target-user fit;
- reply rate;
- interview acceptance;
- invite acceptance;
- activation after invitation;
- retention after activation.

## Common mistakes

- celebrating raw email count;
- collecting no segmentation data;
- leaving the list untouched for months;
- assuming stated willingness to pay equals real payment;
- inviting everyone before the product can support them.

## Revalidate stale waitlists

If the list is old, contact a sample again and confirm the problem/interest still exists before interpreting the original count as current demand.
