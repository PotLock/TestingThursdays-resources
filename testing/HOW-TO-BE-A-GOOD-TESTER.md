# How to Be a Good Tester

The goal is to discover how the product behaves, not prove that it works.

## Core rules

1. Understand the mission before testing.
2. Use the product naturally.
3. Think aloud.
4. Describe observations before giving solutions.
5. Do not ask the founder for help too quickly.
6. Separate bugs from confusion, trust, desirability, and feature requests.
7. Record exact wording when useful.
8. Try recovery paths, not only happy paths.
9. Reproduce serious problems.
10. Leave evidence.

## Better feedback

Weak:

> Navigation is bad.

Better:

> I wanted to start the transfer. I first looked under Wallet and History, then returned Home after about 30 seconds and found Send.

The second statement gives the founder a real observation.
