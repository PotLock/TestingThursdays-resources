# Tester Levels

## Level 0 — Opinion giver

Mostly says what they like/dislike.

## Level 1 — Observer

Can describe what happened without immediately prescribing.

## Level 2 — Structured tester

Can follow missions, reproduce issues, and submit high-quality feedback.

## Level 3 — Exploratory tester

Can systematically test boundaries, state transitions, recovery, and edge cases.

## Level 4 — Domain reviewer

Brings credible specialized product/domain knowledge.

## Level 5 — Test facilitator

Can moderate others, protect test integrity, synthesize evidence, and train new testers.

Progress should be demonstrated through useful contributions, not attendance alone.
