# Screenshot Audit Prompt

```text
You are seeing this product for the first time. Do not assume what it does.
Based only on the screenshots:

1. What do you think this product does?
2. Who do you think it is for?
3. What would you click first?
4. What appears interactive?
5. What is confusing or inconsistent?
6. What appears visually clipped, overloaded, or low hierarchy?
7. What would create hesitation or distrust?
8. What do you expect to happen next?
9. Identify missing loading, empty, failure, or recovery states suggested by the journey.
10. Do not suggest new features unless a specific observed problem requires one.

Separate observations from guesses.
```
