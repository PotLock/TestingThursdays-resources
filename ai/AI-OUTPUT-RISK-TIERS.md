# AI Output Risk Tiers

## Low risk

Formatting, summarizing non-sensitive notes, generating alternative wording.

## Medium risk

UX critique, test generation, non-critical code suggestions, research clustering.

Require verification.

## High risk

Authentication, authorization, payments, sensitive data, production migrations, security, regulated or consequential user outcomes.

Require deterministic tests, careful human review, and specialist review where appropriate. Do not rely on AI confidence statements.
