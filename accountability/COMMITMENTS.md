# Founder Commitments

Commitments should be small enough to complete within the week and specific enough to verify.

Weak:

> Work on onboarding.

Strong:

> Release the microphone-permission recovery fix to the closed beta by Tuesday and observe five first-time users before Thursday.

## Next session

Start with binary status:

**SHIPPED / NOT SHIPPED**

Then diagnose why.

Missed commitments are not shame events; they are evidence about scope, dependencies, prioritization, or execution.
