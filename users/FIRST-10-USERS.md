## Recruiting ladder

1. warm contacts who genuinely fit;
2. communities where the problem is already discussed;
3. direct outreach to people expressing the problem;
4. partnerships with organizations that already reach the user;
5. content and organic discovery;
6. paid acquisition after messaging and activation are understood.

## Outreach principle

Ask for a small, honest action. Example:

> I’m testing an early product for people who struggle with [specific problem]. I’m looking for five people who fit that situation to try one task and tell me where it fails. Interested?
