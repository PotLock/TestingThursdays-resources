# User Access Score

Founders should measure whether they can actually reach the people they claim to serve.

Rate access:

- **5 — Direct:** you can contact many target users today.
- **4 — Warm:** you have reliable introductions.
- **3 — Community:** you know groups/channels where they gather.
- **2 — Paid:** reachable mainly through recruiting/ad platforms.
- **1 — Difficult:** fragmented or gatekept audience.
- **0 — Unknown:** no credible access path.

If user access is weak, fixing distribution/research access may be more important than building additional features.
