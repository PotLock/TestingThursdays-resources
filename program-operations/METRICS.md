# Program Metrics

Do not make attendance the North Star.

## Primary

- median time from joining → first target-user exposure;
- median time → beta/launch;
- percentage of projects moving at least one meaningful stage;
- percentage returning with evidence.

## Weekly supporting metrics

- projects tested;
- target-user tests;
- blockers resolved;
- things shipped;
- experiments completed;
- user/customer conversations;
- commitments completed;
- launches.

## Longer-term

- retained products;
- paying customers/revenue milestones where founders voluntarily report aggregates;
- collaborations created;
- alumni contributions;
- chapters replicated.

Avoid collecting unnecessary personal or sensitive business information.
