# Common Founder Pitfalls

## Before launch

- building before identifying a target user;
- “everyone is our customer”;
- over-polishing prototypes;
- endless rewrites/refactors;
- switching stacks repeatedly;
- building admin infrastructure too early;
- no user-access plan;
- treating waitlist size as validation;
- avoiding sales/outreach;
- assuming more features solve weak demand;
- waiting for a giant public launch.

## After launch

- building immediately instead of observing;
- measuring signups but not activation;
- measuring activation but not retention;
- scaling traffic into a broken funnel;
- listening only to loud users;
- no support synthesis;
- no cohort analysis;
- changing multiple major variables simultaneously.

## Scaling

- premature hiring;
- premature infrastructure complexity;
- paid acquisition before economics/retention are understood;
- automating processes that have not been learned manually;
- founder bottlenecks that are not documented/delegated;
- scaling support load without capacity.
