# Scaling Sessions

Do not scale by making the main stage longer.

Suggested pattern:

- **5–10 people:** one group.
- **10–20:** 2–3 tables.
- **20–40:** multiple breakouts/tracks.
- **40–80:** dedicated tracks with captains.
- **80+:** multiple simultaneous sessions, leads, and specialist tables.

Quality depends more on small-group facilitation and routing than total attendance.

Useful roles at scale:

- lead facilitator;
- table captains;
- recorder;
- timekeeper;
- technical floater;
- GTM/sales captain;
- target-user recruiter;
- specialist reviewers where available.
