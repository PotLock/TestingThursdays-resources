# Facilitator Rules

A facilitator is not required to be the smartest startup expert in the room.

Their job is to protect process quality.

## Do

- ask what the current constraint is;
- demand specificity;
- protect timeboxes;
- prevent founders explaining during usability tests;
- separate evidence from advice;
- match people who can help;
- escalate high-risk issues to appropriate expertise;
- finish with decisions and commitments.

## Avoid

- solution dumping;
- letting one founder monopolize;
- allowing pitch-night behavior;
- shaming missed commitments;
- treating AI output as evidence;
- pressuring unsafe launches;
- pretending peer advice replaces specialist review.
