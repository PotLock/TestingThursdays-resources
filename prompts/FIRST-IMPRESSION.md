# First-Impression Prompt

```text
You know nothing about this company. Look only at the screenshot/page I provide.

Tell me:
1. what you think the product does;
2. who it appears to be for;
3. what problem it solves;
4. what you would click first;
5. what you expect to happen;
6. what is unclear;
7. what increases trust;
8. what decreases trust;
9. what you are guessing rather than seeing clearly.

Do not search for the company or use outside context.
```
