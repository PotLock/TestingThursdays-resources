# B2C: First 100 Users

The objective is not “do marketing.” It is to learn which specific channel and message produce qualified activation.

## Sources to test

- direct outreach;
- niche communities;
- local or interest-based events;
- universities/associations where relevant;
- creator partnerships;
- problem-focused content;
- referrals from early users;
- search intent / SEO pages;
- competitor review mining;
- relevant newsletters or communities.

## Channel scorecard

For each channel track:

```text
Audience
Message
Contacts / impressions
Replies / clicks
Visits
Signups
Activated
Returned
Paid
Cost
Time spent
Learning
KEEP / CHANGE / STOP
```

Optimize for quality of activated/retained users, not raw traffic.
