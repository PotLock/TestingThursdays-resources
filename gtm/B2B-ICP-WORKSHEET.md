# B2B ICP Worksheet

Do not use “businesses” as an ICP.

Define:

- industry;
- company size;
- geography only if operationally relevant;
- current workflow/tool;
- painful event;
- primary user;
- champion;
- buyer;
- trigger event;
- budget source;
- switching cost;
- likely objection;
- why your team can access this market.

## Account list

Build 25–50 accounts matching the ICP before broad outbound.

Track:

