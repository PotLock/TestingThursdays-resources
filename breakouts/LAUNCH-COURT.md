# Launch Court

Use when a founder says “we are not ready.”

Ask:

- What exactly is broken?
- Does it prevent core value?
- Is it unsafe or noncompliant?
- Can it be handled manually?
- Can exposure be limited?
- Can the risk be controlled?

Possible outcomes:

- **LAUNCH** — no legitimate blocker.
- **CONTROLLED BETA** — useful exposure with limits.
- **FIX FIRST** — concrete blocker identified.
- **DO NOT LAUNCH AFFECTED JOURNEY** — critical safety/security/data/compliance issue.
