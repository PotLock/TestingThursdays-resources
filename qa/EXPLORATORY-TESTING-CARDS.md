# Exploratory Testing Cards

Randomly assign one mission to a tester.

- **Bad Network:** interrupt or throttle connectivity in a safe environment.
- **No Permissions:** deny microphone/camera/location/notifications where relevant.
- **Wrong Inputs:** empty, invalid, long, unexpected values.
- **Tiny Screen:** use the smallest supported viewport/device.
- **Old User:** use an account with historical data.
- **New User:** fresh install/account.
- **Interrupted Flow:** background, refresh, lock, restart.
- **Recovery:** intentionally trigger an error and try to recover.
- **Trust:** focus only on moments of hesitation/confidence.
- **Keyboard/Accessibility:** navigate without relying on mouse/touch where relevant.
