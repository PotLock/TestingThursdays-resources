# Risk-Based QA Prioritization

Prioritize in this order unless product-specific risk demands otherwise:

1. safety/security/unauthorized access;
2. money and data integrity;
3. core journey completely broken;
4. launch-blocking reliability;
5. activation friction;
6. trust;
7. retention;
8. conversion/revenue;
9. performance;
10. secondary functionality;
11. feature requests;
12. polish.

Frequency matters, but consequence matters too. A rare duplicate financial transaction can outrank a frequently disliked icon.
