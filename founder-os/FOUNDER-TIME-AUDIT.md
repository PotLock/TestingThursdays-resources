# Founder Time Audit

Track one week of founder time in these buckets:

- BUILD
- USERS
- GTM
- SALES
- SUPPORT
- OPERATIONS
- FUNDRAISING
- HIRING
- ADMIN
- MEETINGS
- LEARNING

Then ask:

1. What is the current company constraint?
2. Does time allocation match that constraint?
3. Which category is consuming time without changing outcomes?
4. What could be deleted, delegated, automated, or timeboxed?

A technical founder spending nearly all week coding while the company has no user-access strategy probably has a prioritization problem, not an engineering problem.
