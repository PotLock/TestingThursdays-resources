# Weekly Operating Rhythm

A suggested rhythm for a Thursday-centered founder lab.

## Thursday

- report last commitment;
- show evidence;
- diagnose constraint;
- test or investigate;
- make a decision;
- commit to a small outcome.

## Friday

Ship the easiest high-confidence improvement while context is fresh.

## Monday

Short checkpoint: is the commitment still the highest-value priority?

## Tuesday

User/customer outreach day. Do not allow a full week to pass without market contact when market learning is the constraint.

## Wednesday

Evidence deadline. Finish the commitment, collect results, and prepare the artifact for Thursday.

## Thursday

Return with what happened, not what was worked on.
