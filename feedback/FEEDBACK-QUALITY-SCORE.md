# Feedback Quality Score

## 0

“Looks good.”

## 1

“I don't like the button.”

## 2

“I had trouble with navigation.”

## 3

“I wanted to complete X and looked in Y first.”

## 4

Includes task, observed path, hesitation/recovery, and expected behavior.

## 5

Level 4 plus environment/evidence/reproduction and tester relevance.

Testing Thursdays should train participants toward Level 4–5 observations.
