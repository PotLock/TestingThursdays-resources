# Severity

## P0 — Critical

Safety/security/unauthorized access, serious money/data integrity, or similarly critical failure.

## P1 — Launch blocker

Core journey cannot reliably complete.

## P2 — Major friction

Product works but significantly harms activation, trust, conversion, or repeated use.

## P3 — Improvement

Noticeable issue but does not materially block core value.

## P4 — Polish

Minor visual/copy/detail issue.

Prioritize with severity, frequency, core-journey importance, evidence strength, and cost/risk—not votes alone.
