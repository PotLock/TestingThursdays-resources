# Community Conduct

Testing Thursdays should be useful, respectful, and evidence-driven.

- Critique products and processes, not people.
- Do not shame founders for failed experiments or missed commitments.
- Do not pressure anyone to expose private, sensitive, or confidential data.
- Do not attempt unauthorized access, security bypasses, fraud, or unsafe testing.
- Do not record participants without appropriate awareness and consent.
- Respect confidentiality requests.
- Avoid monopolizing sessions or using the event primarily for unsolicited selling.
- When expertise is insufficient, say so and escalate rather than bluffing.

The goal is to create a culture where rigorous feedback and failed hypotheses are safe to share.
