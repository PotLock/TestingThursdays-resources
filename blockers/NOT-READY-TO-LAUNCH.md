# Not Ready to Launch?

Do not default to indefinite building. Choose the highest safe exposure level.

1. problem validation;
2. landing page;
3. waitlist;
4. clickable prototype;
5. concierge MVP;
6. internal alpha;
7. invite-only alpha;
8. closed beta;
9. public beta;
10. production.

## Separate unfinished work

### MUST FIX BEFORE EXPOSURE

Safety, security, data integrity, required compliance, broken core value.

### CONTROL WITH LIMITED BETA

Capacity, rough onboarding, known manageable reliability issues.

### MANUAL FOR NOW

Low-volume operations that do not compromise safety/compliance.

### AFTER LAUNCH

Nonessential polish, secondary features, speculative systems.
