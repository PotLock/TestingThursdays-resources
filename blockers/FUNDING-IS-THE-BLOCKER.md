# “Funding Is the Blocker” Diagnostic

Ask:

1. What exactly would the money buy?
2. What milestone would that create?
3. Why is that milestone impossible without the money?
4. Could scope be reduced?
5. Could part of the workflow be manual?
6. Can an existing tool replace custom work?
7. Could customers/pilots fund part of the next milestone?
8. Is the real constraint engineering, user access, distribution, team capacity, or compliance?

Sometimes funding is genuinely the constraint. Often it is a label covering another problem.
